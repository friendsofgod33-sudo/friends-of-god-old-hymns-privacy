# Privacy Policy – Friends of God Old Hymns

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Friends of God Old Hymns ("the app") lets you read, hear and sing old hymns. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: **we do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information.**

## What we collect
Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. We do not ask for your name, location, contacts, photos or microphone.

## What stays on your phone
These are saved only on your device and never sent to us:
- Your favourite hymns.
- Your player settings (for example tempo and instrument) and whether you have seen the tutorial.
- Files you choose to export (words or music as PDF or text). They are created on your device; you decide where they are saved or which app they are shared with.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes this information.

## Internet use
Hymn words and music are inside the app and work offline. The app uses the internet only when you:
- **Play a "Hear Real Singers" recording.** The audio is downloaded from a public host (Internet Archive or Wikimedia Commons). Those services receive normal technical information such as your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies.
- **Open a link** (Give with PayPal, Give with Venmo, hymn source websites). The link opens in your browser or the relevant app, which has its own privacy policy. Giving is optional, and payments are handled entirely by PayPal or Venmo; we never see your payment details.
- **Choose Email Us.** This opens your email app. If you write to us, we receive your email address and message, and we use them only to reply.

## Background playback
So hymns keep playing when the screen is off, the app runs a media-playback notification. It uses no personal information.

## Children
The app contains no ads, purchases or social features and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same web address with a new date.
